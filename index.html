I am a Ph.D. student in Industrial and Systems Engineering at KAIST, advised by Prof. Hwanjun Song.

My current research interest is AI for Research: using AI agents to support and automate research workflows, including peer review and scientific writing. I am particularly interested in how AI systems use evidence and citations in scientific writing.

Previously, I worked on summarization and alignment, with a focus on faithful summarization, fine-grained feedback, and preference data. I received my M.S. in Industrial and Systems Engineering from KAIST in 2026 and my B.S. in Industrial Engineering from UNIST in 2024.

Research interests

  • AI for Research — AI agents for peer review and scientific writing.
  • Evidence-grounded generation — citation fidelity and reliable use of evidence.
  • Summarization and alignment — factual consistency, feedback, and preference learning.
